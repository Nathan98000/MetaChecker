## 4. SHARED-CONTROL SPLITTING
Methods verbatim (p3, "Data items and statistics"): "In studies with multiple intervention arms the sam­ple size of the shared group was split according to the Cochrane Handbook [51] and Rücker et al. [55] to avoid "double-counting" of participants (unit-of-analysis error). For the meta-analysis, 41 study arms were eligible."
Figure-caption verbatim (Figs 2–7, pp22–26, wording near-identical in each): "In multi-arm trials, the sample size of the shared control group was divided to prevent double counting" (Fig 2 caption reads "...the sample size of the shared control group was divided to prevent double counting").
Also relevant (p3): "For the meta-analysis of cross-over trials, the mean difference (MD) and standard error (SE) were calculated according to the Cochrane Handbook for Sys­tematic Reviews of Interventions [51]. If the studies did not provide sufficient data from a paired analysis, a correlation coefficient of 0.5 was set."
Split arithmetic examples (Table 1 n → forest-plot control n):
- Ajibewa 2017 NW (Table 1 n=26; 3 arms): Fig 2 control n=4+4+4... no — NW control 8 per arm (~25/3), intervention 25 per arm; OB (n=13; 3 arms): control 4 per arm (~13/3), intervention 13. In Fig 4 (2 low/mod arms per subgroup) control re-split to 12 (~25/2) and 6 (~13/2); the lone high-intensity arm gets the full 25/13.
- Nemet 2010 (n=22 per weight group; 3 arms each): Fig 2 control 7 per arm (~22/3); Fig 4 (2 arms in low/mod) control 11 (22/2); single aerobic arm in high gets full 22.
- Bozinovski 2009 (n=29; 2 arms): control 14 per arm (~29/2). Thivel 2012 (n=15; 2 arms): control 7 (~15/2) in Fig 2; unsplit 15 with recomputed SE (163.63 / 179.19) in Fig 4 where its arms fall in different subgroups. Saunders (n=20): 10+10. Fillon 2020 (n=18): 9+9. Fillon Mathieu (n=15): 7+7. Masurier (n=20): 10+10. Thivel 2015 (n=14): 7+7.
- Arithmetic check: Fig 2 control column sums to 478 and intervention to 780, exactly matching the text "41 study arms (exercise n = 780 and control n = 478)" (p17 and Abstract p1).

## 5. CONTRADICTIONS / ODDITIES
1. Thivel 2015 rows in Fig 4 disagree with all other figures: Fig 4 prints rugby SE 142.95, CI [-520.18, 40.18] and 75% SE 99.03, CI [-589.10, -200.90]; Figs 2, 3, 5, 6, 7 print rugby SE 121.68, CI [-478.49, -1.51] and 75% SE 142.95, CI [-675.18, -114.82]. Both arms sit in the same Fig 4 subgroup (control n unchanged at 7), so no re-split explains it; the rugby row's Fig 4 SE equals the 75% row's SE elsewhere — likely value misassignment. In Fig 4 the 75% CI excludes 0 far more strongly.
2. Varley Campbell 2017 NW + Snack: Fig 5 prints SE 98.8, CI [-276.64, 110.64]; Figs 2/3/4/7 print SE 98.81, CI [-276.66, 110.66].
3. Thivel 2013 OB 30min 75%: Fig 6 prints SE 115.24, CI [254.13, 705.87]; Figs 2/3/4/5/7 print SE 115.25, CI [254.11, 705.89].
4. Fig 4 has NO printed overall Total row — an unlabeled overall diamond is drawn above the axis with only the subgroup-difference test text (Chi²=0.01, df=1, P=0.94). All other subgroup figures (5, 6, 7) print a labeled Total row.
5. Control totals differ across figures because splits are recomputed per subgroup: Fig 2/5/7 control total 478; Fig 4 subtotal control n sum 359+219=578; Fig 6 219. Intervention totals stay consistent (780; Fig 4: 547+233=780; Fig 6: 233).
6. Text (p17) says "a total number of 43 exercise conditions" but only 41 arms enter the meta-analysis; the two absent conditions are Thivel 2014's OB arm (Table 1 p14 marks it "* study arm from Thivel et al. 2013" — duplicate data) and Thivel 2020's second arm (snack energy-replacement after-load, Table 1 p16). Neither appears in any forest plot.
7. Spelling inconsistencies: forest plots consistently print "Bozinosvki" (verified at 10x zoom in Figs 2, 3, 7) vs "Bozinovski" in text/Table 1/Table 2; "Saunders 2013 NW wallking + PA" [sic]; all six forest plots' column header reads "Invervention" [sic] instead of "Intervention"; axis label "HIgher EI in CON" [sic capital I].
8. Ajibewa 2017: Table 1 reports NW n=26, but forest plots use intervention Total 25 per NW arm (control 8) — a 1-participant discrepancy between Table 1 and the plots (AMBIGUOUS whether dropout or split rounding).
9. Saunders labeled "2013" in plots/Table 1/Table 2, but reference [70] is Br J Nutr 2014;111:747–54 (published 2014).
10. Table 2 caption begins "Table 2 2 Risk of bias" (duplicated "2"); its column order is D1, DS, D2, D3, D4, D5, Overall (DS = the crossover-specific domain, placed between D1 and D2). RoB counts in Table 2 (7 low / 11 some concerns / 4 high: Ajibewa, Fearnbach 2016, Morris, Nemet) match the text percentages on p18 and match the 14 arms excluded from Fig 3 (41-14=27, df=26 ✓).
11. Table 1 (p13, Thivel et al. 2012): Fat (%) CON printed as "20.72 ± 4.69" — identical to Protein (%) CON, and the three CON macronutrient percentages then sum to ~76%, suggesting a copy error in Table 1 (this is the table the separate publisher correction addresses; correction not in this file). Also Fig 2 label "Thivel 2013 OB 30min 75%" says "30min" while Table 1 describes 3 x 10 min bouts.
12. Nothing UNRESOLVED except the missing Fig 4 Total values (item 4) — every study-row number in Figs 2–7 was legible at zoom.
