## 4. SHARED-CONTROL SPLITTING

Methods, p3 (verbatim): "In studies with multiple intervention arms the sample size of the shared group was split according to the Cochrane Handbook [51] and Rücker et al. [55] to avoid 'double-counting' of participants (unit-of-analysis error). For the meta-analysis, 41 study arms were eligible." Related, p3: "For the meta-analysis of cross-over trials, the mean difference (MD) and standard error (SE) were calculated according to the Cochrane Handbook for Systematic Reviews of Interventions [51]. If the studies did not provide sufficient data from a paired analysis, a correlation coefficient of 0.5 was set." Every forest-plot caption repeats (pp22–26): "In multi-arm trials, the sample size of the shared control group was divided to prevent double counting."

Split arithmetic examples (Table 1 n → forest-plot control n): Ajibewa NW n=26, 3 arms → control 8/8/8 in Fig 2 (exercise printed 25, not 26); in Fig 4 only 2 NW arms share the low/mod subgroup → 12/12, and the lone high-intensity arm gets 25 (unsplit). Ajibewa OB 13 → 4/4/4 (Fig 2), 6/6 + 13 (Fig 4). Nemet 22 per group → 7/7/7 (Fig 2), 11/11 (Fig 4, two arms/subgroup), 22 (Fig 6, one arm). Bozinovski 29 → 14/14. Thivel 2012 n=15 → 7/7 (Fig 2), but 15 each in Fig 4 (arms fall in different subgroups). Thivel 2015 n=14 → 7/7. Fillon 2020 18 → 9/9; Fillon Mathieu 15 → 7/7; Masurier 20 → 10/10; Saunders 20 → 10/10. SEs are correspondingly recomputed per analysis (see flagged Fig 4 rows). Splits round DOWN (26→24 total, 13→12, 29→28, 15→14), so summed control n (478) undercounts.

## 5. CONTRADICTIONS/ODDITIES

1. Sign convention: plotted MD = Control − Exercise (positive = "HIgher EI in CON"); verified against Table 1 means. The abstract's "MD = 23.31 [-27.54, 74.15] kcal" for exercise-vs-control is easily misread as EX−CON.
2. Thivel 2015 SE swap in Fig 4: rugby arm SE 142.95 / CI [-520.18, 40.18] and 75% arm SE 99.03 / CI [-589.10, -200.90], versus SE 121.68 / [-478.49, -1.51] and 142.95 / [-675.18, -114.82] in Figs 2, 5, 6, 7. In Fig 4 the 75% arm's CI stays entirely below zero but the rugby arm's no longer does. Consequently Fig 4's high-intensity pool (22.71 [-78.48, 123.90], I²=82%) ≠ Fig 6's pool of the identical 14 arms (27.25 [-69.66, 124.15], I²=80%).
3. Fig 4 has an overall diamond but NO printed "Total (95% CI)" row (values unrecoverable from the figure).
4. Rounding inconsistencies between figures for identical rows: Varley Campbell 2017 NW + Snack SE 98.8 / CI [-276.64, 110.64] in Fig 5 vs 98.81 / [-276.66, 110.66] in Figs 2/3/7; Thivel 2013 SE 115.24 / CI [254.13, 705.87] in Fig 6 vs 115.25 / [254.11, 705.89] in Figs 2/4/7.
5. Ajibewa NW: Table 1 says n=26 but all forest plots print exercise n=25 (and splits 8+8+8=24 or 12+12+25).
6. Text (p17) says 43 exercise conditions with 13 (30%) high intensity, yet Fig 4/Fig 6's high-intensity subgroup contains 14 of the 41 pooled arms (and the excluded Thivel 2014 OB arm was also 75-percent-intensity), so the 13/43 count is inconsistent with the figures. (Also "26 (60%)" — 26/43 = 60% ✓; but 13/43 = 30.2% and 4/43 = 9.3% are fine individually; the count itself is the problem.)
7. Country percentages (p4): "Europe (n = 17; 67%)" — 17/22 = 77%, not 67%; "Canada and USA (both n = 2; 13%)" — 4/22 = 18% (2/22 = 9%); Israel (Nemet) unmentioned in the tally.
8. Typos: column header "Invervention" in every forest plot; "Saunders 2013 NW wallking + PA"; axis label "HIgher EI in CON"; forest plots spell "Bozinosvki" (Table 1/Table 2/refs: "Bozinovski"); Table 2 caption reads "Table 2 2 Risk of bias"; Fig 1 box "Records identified from*".
9. Table 2 has six domain columns — D1, DS, D2, D3, D4, D5 — but the caption defines only D1–D5 ("DS" = RoB 2 crossover Domain S, period/carryover, undefined in caption). Overall RoB: high = Ajibewa 2017, Fearnbach 2016*, Morris 2018, Nemet 2010 (matches "four studies (18%)"); low = Fearnbach Masterson 2016, Fillon 2020, Fillon Beaulieu 2020*, Fillon Mathieu 2020, Masurier 2018, Miguet 2018, Thivel 2020 (7 = "31%"); some concerns = remaining 11 ("50%").
10. Thivel 2014 OB arm and Thivel 2020 second (snack-replacement) arm appear in Table 1 but not in any forest plot (43 conditions → 41 pooled); Table 1 flags the Thivel 2014 OB energy-intake row "EX: 1306 ± 304 *" as "* study arm from Thivel et al. 2013" (Fig-2 row uses 1307 via Thivel 2013).
11. Fig 3's caption says "low or moderate risk of bias" though RoB 2 categories are low/some concerns/high. Text claim "I² increased to 69% (Fig. 3)" matches the figure.
12. Machine-readability: body text, Table 1, and all captions are extractable text; Figs 1–9 and the Table 2 grid are vector Form XObjects whose glyphs are NOT extractable (no embedded raster images either) — all figure values above were read visually from rendered images.
